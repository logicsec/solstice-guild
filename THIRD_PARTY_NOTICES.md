# WoWSims Forever item data

The planner item catalog is derived from https://github.com/wowsims/forever/blob/57df2a7a5a2bb085bfce15890a33d655bb7fd66f/assets/database/db.json (retrieved November 23, 2026). Item levels and stat totals use the baseline scaling option; random suffixes, buffs, enchants and simulation effects are not included.
